Alphabetical, as Today already sorts its Publications. The row scrolls; nothing else moves with it.
The monogram rule Initials of the first two significant words, uppercased. Articles and particles are dropped (la, il, the, of, dello), digits are skipped, an internal capital splits a compound. One significant word left gives its first two letters. ANSA → AN · la Repubblica → RE · Il Sole 24 Ore → SO
London Review of Books → LR · TechRadar → TR
